## Emblem

The Wings of Freedom / Survey Corps emblem is used in the header, hero, contact section, and browser icon. Source: [PNGAAA asset page](https://www.pngaaa.com/detail/1697325), [original PNG download](https://www.pngaaa.com/api-download/1697325). The original transparent 521 × 707 PNG is stored unchanged as `assets/images/wings-of-freedom.png`; the site's checkerboard preview was not used. The emblem belongs to Attack on Titan's respective rights holders.
