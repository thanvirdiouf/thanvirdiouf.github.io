01 / ABOUT

A little closer
to the machine.

I like understanding the systems I use, changing how they work, and building things that fit the way I live.

That means Linux kernel work for the POCO X5 5G and Acer Nitro 5, experiments with postmarketOS, and reproducible setups with Nix. On the desktop, it means custom window managers, status bars, and themes.

I also build Android apps for timers, sleep journaling, puzzles, and keeping a record of everyday progress. Another thread leads into scientific computing: simulating how Inconel 617's microstructure changes during deformation, and exploring neural networks in C.
